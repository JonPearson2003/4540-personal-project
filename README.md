# BuildTrack

## Elevator Pitch
BuildTrack helps construction crews manage the everyday details of their jobs from a
phone. Workers can clock in and out against a project, photograph receipts and invoices, record
materials used, and request supplies. Project managers can document job progress with
photos, respond to material requests, and review project activity in one place.
## Problem the app solves
Construction businesses often have important project information scattered across paper
timesheets, text messages, camera rolls, spreadsheets, and receipts stored in trucks or folders.
Workers may call or text managers when they need more supplies, and progress photos may be
mixed in with personal pictures without clear project labels. This makes it difficult to answer
basic questions: Who worked on a project and for how long? What materials were purchased or
used? What supplies are needed next? What work has been completed?
## Why the problem is important
The idea for this app was given to me by my brother who works in construction as a
project manager. After talking with him about why he would want an app like this he explained
that labor, materials, and project coordination all affect construction costs and schedules.
Incomplete records can lead to forgotten hours, duplicate purchases, supply shortages, delayed
work, or confusion about project status. Capturing information at the job site such as when work
happens, materials are used, or a purchase is made can make records more accurate and
easier to review.
## How users currently solve the problem
Depending on the construction company or contractor they may use paper timesheets or
a time-clock app for hours, a phone camera for receipts and progress pictures, text messages
for supply requests, and spreadsheets or even handwritten lists for inventory. These tools can
work separately, but they do not automatically connect a worker's hours, a material request, a
receipt, and a progress photo to the correct project.
## How your app improves the experience
BuildTrack organizes information around projects. Workers can clock in, select a project,
attach purchases and photos to the relevant job, record materials as they are used, and submit
requests when supplies are needed. Managers can review and respond to requests, document
progress over time, and see project activity without searching through separate messages and
files.
